FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

When and how are meals delivered?

Your full day's meals are delivered between 6 and 8 AM in an insulated bag with ice packs. You can also choose the evening slot (5–7 PM) to receive the next day's meals.

Is all the food halal?

Yes. All our meat and chicken is 100% halal and sourced from certified local suppliers.

Can I pause or cancel my plan?

Yes. Pause anytime from the app or WhatsApp with 24 hours' notice. Paused days are added to the end of your plan. There are no long-term contracts.

Can I remove ingredients I'm allergic to?

Yes. Tell us your allergies (nuts, dairy, gluten, shellfish and more) at signup and we'll swap dishes automatically.

How do I pay?

We accept debit/credit cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer and cash on delivery for the first order.

How do I heat the meals?

Our containers are microwave-safe. Heat for 2–3 minutes. Salads and breakfast items like overnight oats are served cold.

What happens to the containers?

Leave them outside the next morning and our rider will collect them for recycling. Return 20 containers and get a free snack.
